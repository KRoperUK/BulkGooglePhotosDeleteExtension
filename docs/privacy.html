No data collected

Privacy Policy

Bulk Delete Google Photos · Effective 23 October 2026

Short version: this extension collects nothing, transmits nothing, and stores nothing. It has no server. Everything it does happens inside your browser, on the Google Photos page you already have open.

1. What the extension does

Bulk Delete Google Photos is a browser extension that automates the Google Photos web interface so that photos can be selected and deleted in bulk. It adds a control panel to photos.google.com and, when you start a run, clicks the same interface elements you would click yourself.

2. Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, request, or record any personal information. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • your name, email address, or account identifiers;
  • your photos, videos, or any file content;
  • your browsing history or activity on any website;
  • device identifiers, location, or usage analytics;
  • crash reports or diagnostic data.

3. Data the extension reads locally

To function, the extension reads the structure of the Google Photos page in your browser — the same page content your browser already displays — to locate selection checkboxes, the Delete button, and the confirmation dialog. This processing happens entirely in memory in your browser. It is never copied out of the page, and it never leaves your device. The extension does not open, decode, or inspect the contents of your photos.

4. Network activity

The extension makes no network requests of any kind. It contains no fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket or sendBeacon calls, no remote script loading, and no remote code execution. All logic ships inside the installed package. There is no backend service, no API, and no analytics endpoint for it to talk to.

5. Permissions

The extension's manifest requests no permissions at all. It declares a single content script limited to https://photos.google.com/*. It cannot read or modify any other website, and it holds no access to your file system, tabs, history, or bookmarks.

6. Storage

No data is stored. The extension does not use localStorage, IndexedDB, cookies, or any browser storage API. Settings you type into the panel exist only until the page is closed.

7. Third parties

There are no third parties. Nothing is sold, rented, shared, or transferred to anyone, and no third-party SDKs, trackers, or advertising services are included.

8. Deletions and Google's services

When you ask the extension to delete photos, it performs those actions through Google Photos in your browser while you are signed in. The deletions are carried out by Google's own service, under your Google account, and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Deleted items are moved to the Google Photos Bin, where Google retains them for a period before permanent deletion. This extension has no access to that data beyond the visible interface.

9. Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. Because the extension collects no data, any future change that introduced data collection would require an explicit new permission request, which you would see and could refuse at install or update time.
